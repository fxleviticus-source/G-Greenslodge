FAQ

Before you book.

Do you offer airport pickup?

Free airport pickup is available on request at the Lusaka branch. Add it to your booking request and we’ll confirm the details.

Is breakfast included?

Lusaka lists a full English breakfast with rooms. Inclusions can differ by branch and rate, so we confirm them when we reply to your request.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Times differ slightly by branch. Tell us your expected arrival in the booking form and we’ll confirm everything with your reply.

Can I host a conference, workshop or party?

Yes. Solwezi has a conference hall for workshops and meetings, and all branches welcome celebrations. Use the Event tab on the booking page.

How does booking work?

Choose your branch, dates, guests and room, then send the request. It opens WhatsApp to our main line with everything filled in. The team confirms availability and rates — nothing is charged online.

Can I bring children or pets?

Families are welcome. Pet policies differ by branch, so please ask when you book.
