Main branch

Lusaka — Lilayi

Lusaka Province

Our home base: chalet-style rooms tucked into lush gardens, a pool, a restaurant and bar, and the Pub N' Grill where live artists play.

  • Chalet-style rooms with air-conditioning, fridge and a tea & coffee station
  • Swimming pool and garden setting
  • Restaurant, bar and Pub N' Grill — live artists on selected nights
  • Free airport pickup on request
  • Free internet and DSTV

Kazungula

Southern Province · Zambezi border

A comfortable stop at the gateway to the Zambezi, the Kazungula border crossing and Botswana’s Chobe — with a restaurant, bar and weekend buzz.

  • Air-conditioned rooms with private bathrooms, TV and kettle
  • Free Wi-Fi and free on-site parking
  • 24-hour front desk
  • Restaurant, bar and cocktail nights
  • Vegetarian, halal and vegan options on request

Four taps to your stay.
